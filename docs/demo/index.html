▸ Key Technical Concepts in Plain Language (Click to expand)
Demarchy / Stratified Sortition (Aristotle)Citizen councils chosen by cryptographic lottery across demographic/occupational quotas instead of capital-funded elections, with mandatory post-term quarantine.
Mechanism Design (Hurwicz–Maskin)Designing institutional rules (zero CEOs, unit vote weight, zero re-selection) so self-interested actors cannot capture political power or hoard surplus.
Arrow vs. Black Social ChoiceMulti-candidate elections suffer voting paradoxes; continuous 1D budget shares (% Health vs % Education) guarantee a unique, stable median-voter equilibrium.
Hoeffding Pivotal-Ballot BoundA single ballot's chance of deciding a mass election is ~10^(-13,723), whereas a sorted chamber of size n converges exponentially to the public median.
Sparse Leontief Input–Output (CSR)Stores only real physical supply links (~20–100 inputs per product) to compute exact gross output x = (I-A)^(-1)y* across millions of goods in seconds.
Cockshott Labour-Time (H), p/v & HarmonyCounts total direct + indirect work hours per unit (H), adjusts targets via real-time demand/labour ratio (p/v), and smooths capacity bottlenecks (Harmony).
Usufruct [1,0,0,0] & Rational 1/N SurplusCooperatives use public equipment (Use=1) without selling, buying, or inheriting firms (0,0,0), splitting 100% of net surplus equally per worked day ($0.00 retained).
Common Random Numbers (CRN) CounterfactualsTests Regimes A, B, and C under the exact same yearly climate and economic shocks so every divergence reflects institutional design alone.
